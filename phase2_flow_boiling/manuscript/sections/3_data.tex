\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Sources and harmonisation}

We assembled 351 ONB points from nine experimental studies
(Table~\ref{tab:sources}), digitised from boiling curves, axial
wall-temperature distributions and parity plots. Each record carries fluid,
channel type, hydraulic diameter $\Dh$, mass flux $G$, pressure $P$, inlet (or
local) subcooling $\dTsub$, surface descriptors where available, and the ONB
targets $\qonb$ and/or $\dTonb$. All quantities were converted to SI and to the
naming convention of the modelling code; saturation properties were recomputed
with CoolProp at the reported pressure.

The assembled set spans hydraulic diameters of $0.35$--\SI{9.5}{mm}, pressures
of $0.1$--\SI{16}{MPa}, mass fluxes of $100$--\SI{2000}{kg\,m^{-2}\,s^{-1}} and
inlet subcoolings up to \SI{150}{K}, predominantly for water with a minority of
refrigerant records. To our knowledge this is the widest pressure and
length-scale coverage assembled for a single ONB model
(Fig.~\ref{fig:coverage}); the marginal distributions of the governing
variables are shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:dist}.

\subsection{Curation and pool-boiling decontamination}

Aggregating heterogeneous literature requires care. Several candidate records,
although tabulated as ``ONB'', were on inspection pool-boiling parity data
(no mass flux, no subcooling) or carried unknown pressure that precludes
property evaluation. Including them degraded the model and corrupted the
pressure dependence. We therefore removed (i) multi-fluid pool-boiling parity
points with absent $G$ and $\dTsub$, and (ii) records with unreported pressure,
yielding the final flow-boiling set. Dataset-schema flags retain provenance for
every retained point.

\subsection{Microchannel and high-pressure anchors}

Two additions were made specifically to broaden coverage. The Qu and Mudawar
microchannel data \cite{QUMUDAWAR2002} ($\Dh=\SI{0.349}{mm}$) anchor the
confinement-dominated end; here the ONB occurs near the channel exit, so the
local (outlet) subcooling is used. The Wang et al.\ high-pressure data
\cite{WANG2024} ($P=10$--\SI{16}{MPa}, $60\times\SI{2}{mm}$ channel) anchor the
nuclear end, with ONB identified from the single-phase-to-boiling knee of each
boiling curve; the wall superheat falls from $\sim\!\SI{3.5}{K}$ at
\SI{10}{MPa} to $\sim\!\SI{1.5}{K}$ at \SI{16}{MPa}, a clean
$P\!\uparrow\!\Rightarrow\!\dTonb\!\downarrow$ sequence that the model is asked
to reproduce.
