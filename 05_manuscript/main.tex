\section*{Data availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Data availability}
The curated pool-boiling dataset (\num{1361} boiling-curve points,
\num{82} ONB labels, and \num{49} surface descriptor cards), the
trained PINN models, and the analysis scripts used to produce all
figures and tables will be released as open-source supplementary
material upon acceptance. The repository includes reproducible
notebooks and the per-paper figure-digitization metadata required to
audit the dataset at the figure level.
